Masala Peanut Fry🍜

    Ingredients:

  • 1 handful peanuts
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt
  • Chili powder
  • Curry leaves (optional)

    • Steps:

  • Heat oil in a pan.
  • Add peanuts and fry for 2–3 minutes.
  • Add salt and chili powder.
  • Mix well.
