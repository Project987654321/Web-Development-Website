Cucumber Sandwich🍜

    Ingredients:

  • 2 bread slices
  • ½ cucumber sliced thin
  • Butter or cream cheese
  • Salt and pepper

    • Steps:

  • Spread butter or cream cheese on bread.
  • Add cucumber slices.
  • Sprinkle salt and pepper.
  • Close sandwich.
