Spicy Noodle Fry🍜

    Ingredients:

  • 1 pack noodles
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Chopped onion
  • Chili flakes

    • Steps:

  • Cook noodles normally.
  • Heat oil in a pan.
  • Add onion and cook for 1 minute.
  • Add noodles and chili flakes.
  • Fry for 2 minutes.
