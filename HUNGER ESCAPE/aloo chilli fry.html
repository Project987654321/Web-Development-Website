Aloo Chilli Fry🍜

    Ingredients:

  • 1 boiled potato
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt
  • Chili flakes
  • Optional: soy sauce

    • Steps:

  • Cut potato into cubes.
  • Heat oil in a pan and fry lightly.
  • Sprinkle salt, chili flakes, and soy sauce.
