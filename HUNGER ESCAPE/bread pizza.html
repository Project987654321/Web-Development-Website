Bread Pizza 🍜

    Ingredients:

  • 2 bread slices
  • 1 tbsp pizza sauce or ketchup
  • Chopped onion or capsicum
  • Cheese
  • Chili flakes

    • Steps:

  • Spread sauce on bread.
  • Add onion and capsicum.
  • Sprinkle cheese and chili flakes.
  • Heat on a pan for 3–4 minutes.
