Spicy Corn Chaat🍜

    Ingredients:

  • 1 cup boiled corn
  • 1 tbsp chopped onion
  • Pinch salt
  • Pinch chili powder
  • Lemon juice

    • Steps:

  • Put corn in a bowl.
  • Add onion, salt, and chili powder.
  • Squeeze lemon juice.
  • Mix everything well.
