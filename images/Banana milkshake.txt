<h1>Banana Milk Shake🍜</h1>
<div class="content">

  <div class="text">
    <ul>
      <b style="color:orange;">Ingredients:</b><br><br>
      <li>1 medium ripe Banana</li>
      <li>1 Glass milk (approx. 200-250ml)</li>
      <li>1-2 table spoon honey or sugar</li>
      <li>1 teaspoon peanut butter or cocoa powder
  
      <br>
      <b style="color:orange;">Steps:</b><br><br>
      <li>Peal and break the banana into small pieces in a bowl or glass.</li>
      <li>Smash the banana well with a spoon until it becomes smooth paste.</li>
      <li>Add sugar or honey and mix.</li>
      <li>Pour in the milk slowly while stirring.</li>
      <li>Transfer the mixture to a bottle/shaker and shake for 15-20sec</li>
      <br>
      <b style="color:orange;">Tips:</b><br><br>
      <li>Add pinch of cinnamon.<li>
      <li>Use cold milk for better taste</li>
    </ul>
