Cold Coffee🍜

    Ingredients:

  • 2 tablespoon of boiling water (approx. 200-250ml)
  • 1-2 table spoon instant coffee
  • 1-2 teaspoon sugar/honey
  • Optional: coca powder or chocolate syrup

    • Steps:

  • Add coffee and sugar to a cup or shaker.
  • Pour 2–3 spoons of warm water and mix with same amount of coffee and sugar/honey.
  • Using a hand mixer, you whisk/whip it together for about anywhere from 2 minutes to 5 minutes.
