<h1>Instant Iced Tea recipe🍜</h1>
<div class="content">

  <div class="text">
    <ul>
      <b style="color:orange;">Ingredients:</b><br><br>
      <li>1 glass warm water</li>
      <li>Few drops lemon juice (approx. 200ml)</li>
      <li>1 table spoon honey</li>
      <li>1 tea bag</li>
  
      <br>
      <b style="color:orange;">Steps:</b><br><br>
      <li>Pour warm water into a glass.</li>
      <li>Add honey and mix.</li>
      <li>Add lemon juice and stir.</li>
      <li>Put tea bag in hot water for 2-3 min</li>
      <br>
      <b style="color:orange;">Tips:</b><br><br>
      <li>Good morning drink.</li>
    </ul>
