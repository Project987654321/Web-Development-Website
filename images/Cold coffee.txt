<h1>Cold Coffee🍜</h1>
<div class="content">

  <div class="text">
    <ul>
      <b style="color:orange;">Ingredients:</b><br><br>
      <li>1 Glass milk (approx. 200-250ml)</li>
      <li>1-2 table spoon instant coffee</li>
      <li>1 teaspoon sugar/honey</li>
      <li>Optional: coca powder or chocolate syrup</li> 
  
      <br>
      <b style="color:orange;">Steps:</b><br><br>
      <li>Add coffee and sugar to a bottle or shaker.</li>
      <li>Pour 2–3 spoons of warm water and mix so the coffee dissolves.</li>
      <li>Add cold milk.</li>
      <li>Close the bottle and shake for 20–30 seconds.</li>
      
    </ul>
