Apple Milkshake

    Ingredients:

  • 1 Apple
  • 1 Glass milk (approx. 200ml)
  • 1 table spoon honey or sugar
  • Optional: pinch of cinnamon

    • Steps:

  • Wash and peal the apple.
  • Grate the apple or chop it into pieces.
  • Put the apple pieces in a bowl.
  • Add honey or sugar and mix.
  • Add milk and stir thoroughly.
  • Add milk and stir thoroughly.

    • Tips:

  • Drink immediately so the apple doesn’t turn brown.
